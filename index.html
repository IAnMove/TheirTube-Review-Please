                TheirTube keeps creators connected to revenue.

                TheirTube is the fictional video platform inside the game: an ad network, a payout ledger, a moderation desk, and a corporate myth that keeps operating after humans stop being the center of the process.

                Revenue, safety, anger, morale, and runway are never independent metrics.
